The Second Church of the Kykeon has one criterion for membership: **meditation without effort**, for 15 minutes. You certify this yourself. There is no form to submit. If sitting is a struggle, you are a catechumen. Keep practicing and try again.

## What "effortless" means

Most meditation instruction says to pick a focus, such as the breath, and gently return to it whenever the mind wanders. That is the heart of the practice, and it works. But it can quietly tip into a form of suppression, where uncomfortable parts of the mind are pushed away to keep up the appearance of peace.

The alternative is to notice which part of you pulled your attention away. Keep a notepad and pencil beside you. Jot a brief summary ("worried part concerned about the presentation," "sad part missing Mom"), ask the part to step back for now, and promise to return after the session. Then resume. Review your notes afterward and give those parts the care they were asking for.

Do this consistently and something changes. Parts that have learned you are trustworthy step back willingly when you ask for their cooperation. The sitting stops being a struggle. The less you force it, the more your Self shines through, like a quiet lake reflecting the sky once the ripples settle.

Effortless means the absence of fight, not willpower disguised as calm.[^effortless] Strain marks a divided house, so effortlessness shows your Self is leading. It is a blunt criterion, but better than nothing.[^justification] If you are not at ease yet, give the method a few weeks and sit again.

[^effortless]: For a fuller account of how effortlessness relates to unblending and Self-energy, see [IFS and Meditation](https://partsandself.org/ifs-and-meditation/).

[^justification]: Lovas et al. (2026), a clinical manual on IFS therapy with psychedelics, gives no way to check whether a client has enough access to Self to use psychedelics without a sitter. In [Joshua's review](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/posts/lovas-et-al-2026/), he proposes this criterion to fill that gap. A sitter can help when a part has taken over, but a sitter cannot share the client's altered state, the sober/intoxicated divide invites guru-disciple dynamics, and guided sessions cost more than many can pay.

## The sacrament

*You sit, and the ripples settle. Fifteen minutes slip past unopposed, and only the bell reminds you that time exists. You have met the criterion.*

Congratulations, member! The sacrament, the kykeon of the Eleusinian Mysteries, is now open to you. [Learn about the sacrament.](/sacrament/)
