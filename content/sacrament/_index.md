## Orientation

Kykeon is similar in effect to MDMA, but the euphoria extends far beyond the MDMA ceiling.

Kykeon tolerance is similar to ergine tolerance. It resets in about 4 days (X is a day of use, O is a day without): X O O O O X

Kykeon can be co-administered with [tetrahydroharmine](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/docs/psychoactive/thh/).

## Screen for Contraindications

**This protocol does not risk ergotism.** Ergine belongs to the ergot alkaloids. But ergotism (a potentially fatal condition) requires chronic, heavy exposure far beyond the mild dose here. Ergine is also a much weaker vasoconstrictor than the alkaloids behind classic ergotism. Still, vascular sensitivity varies, so screen yourself first.

Do not proceed if any of these apply:

- Cardiovascular disease, including coronary artery disease, prior heart attack, angina, or uncontrolled hypertension
- Peripheral vascular disease or Raynaud's phenomenon
- Pregnancy, or trying to conceive (ergot alkaloids can contract the uterus)
- Breastfeeding
- Liver impairment, since ergot alkaloids are metabolized there
- Taking strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (certain macrolide antibiotics, azole antifungals, HIV protease inhibitors), which can raise ergot levels dangerously
- Taking triptans or other vasoconstrictors

Stahl himself is a heart attack survivor, yet he reports no vasoconstriction when he uses kykeon. [His ALDH enzyme seems to be unusual](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/posts/kykeon-cold-case/), so his experience may not transfer to you. Do not treat it as evidence that the protocol is safe for a damaged heart.

Ergot alkaloids aren't known to be bioaccumulative. The liver breaks them down (mostly via CYP3A4) and the body clears them within days. Humans have no known delayed illness from an exposure that felt tolerable at the time.

## Extract the Ergine

Even beginning chemists will recognize this as a straightforward LSA extraction.

1. Grind the seeds to a fine powder in the {{< tooltip label="grinder" >}}Stahl adds, "crush the seed fragments by hand with pestle using a twisting downwards motion<br/>for a minute until they look more like dust fragments, don't be afraid to then pound the seed dust<br/>with the pestle to get it crushed as finely as possible."{{< /tooltip >}}.
2. In a shot glass, combine the seed powder with 30mg tartaric acid and the ethanol. Stir for ten minutes.
3. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes (1 hour is better), to let the seed fragments settle out of the solution.
4. Pour only the clear liquid into the baking dish. Discard any cloudy liquid and spent seed matter. Let a dehydrator or hair dryer carry the alcohol away, keeping the temperature below 50°C (122°F).
5. Once the alcohol has fully evaporated, scrape the residue from the dish into an oily blob with a spatula or razor blade.

The extract's weight includes inert oils, not just ergine. Divide the blob into two halves.

**Do not drink alcohol while aldehydes are in play.** Ethanol is metabolized into acetaldehyde, a carcinogen and the main cause of hangover symptoms. The ALDH blockers suppress the enzyme that clears it, so acetaldehyde would linger. Make sure the extraction ethanol has fully evaporated before you ingest the residue.

## Take It

1. Practice meditation to ensure you'll meet the kykeon in the best possible frame of mind. Kykeon isn't like MDMA—indifferent to whatever mood you present.[^mdma] If you only feel buzzing stimulation (without euphoria), consider whether you prepared yourself sufficiently.[^stimulation]
2. Take half of the ergine extract (25 seeds' worth), the barley grass (mixed with a squeeze of lemon juice), and your ALDH blocker all at once. Wash it down with seltzer water.[^seltzer] Starting with half is the safe way in: with 25 seeds' worth of ergine, the worst that can happen is an ordeal you will recover from in 3-4 hours.
3. Wait 90 minutes.
4. If things feel promising, take the other half (ergine only, since the barley grass and ALDH blocker from the first dose are still active).
5. Sip water throughout.
6. If things go wrong (sedation instead of euphoria), move as much as you can to resist sedation and encourage your kidneys to clear residual ergine.[^kidney-clearance] A hot shower can help persuade the bladder to loosen its grip.

## Pharmacokinetics

What follows is a guess built from journey reports,[^pk-credit] implemented as a simulator to make specific predictions. Picture the body as three compartments. The **pre-barrier pool** (stomach plus bloodstream) holds ergine and barley grass aldehydes until they reach the blood-brain barrier. The **blood-brain barrier** either leaves ergine untouched or converts it into kykeon, depending on whether aldehydes are present.[^not-liver] The **brain**, behind the barrier, is where the subjective effects happen.

[^not-liver]: Metabolic conversions like this usually happen in the liver, but Stahl's self-reports can't be reconciled with that account. For most people, ALDH clears the barley grass aldehydes, so the kykeon effect fades and the ergine effect reasserts itself. Stahl's ALDH seems to leave the aldehydes alone, so he feels kykeon throughout and never feels ergine. Yet ergine is certainly in his bloodstream, and ergine can ordinarily pass the blood-brain barrier. That he feels none of its effects points to ergine being converted into kykeon as it enters the brain.

[^pk-credit]: Deepest, most fervent thanks to Hamilton Morris—intrepid psychonaut who marched fearlessly into the ergine-aldehyde unknown so the rest of us wouldn't have to. May his blood-brain barrier be forever blessed. Read the saga in [The Kykeon Cold Case](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/posts/kykeon-cold-case/).

Ergine takes about 30 minutes to get from the stomach into the bloodstream. Blood circulates past the blood-brain barrier repeatedly. Barley grass aldehydes get from the stomach through the bloodstream to the barrier fast, in about 5 minutes. Psychoactive effects depend on travel across the blood-brain barrier. Each pass converts ergine into kykeon only if aldehydes are present at the barrier. Otherwise ergine passes through unchanged. Aldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH) attacks aldehydes, gradually depleting them. Kykeon has a short duration in the brain. Residual ergine is largely cleared by the kidneys,[^kidney-clearance] and that requires drinking water. The simulator is an empirical fit that simplifies this physiology (it treats the barrier as a single pass, for instance), and many of the underlying mechanisms remain a mystery.

## The Case for an RFRA Exception

*RFRA is American law. Other countries regulate ergine under different statutes and recognize religious exemptions differently, if at all.*

Ergine, also called lysergic acid amide or LSA, is regulated under the federal Controlled Substances Act. We contend that this regulation, as applied to the sincere religious use of ergine in the Eleusinian tradition, merits an exception under the Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA).

### The argument in brief

1. **Sincere religious exercise.** The kykeon is the sacrament at the center of the Eleusinian mysteries, one of the longest-lived religious traditions of the ancient world. A church devoted to that tradition exercises religion when it seeks to revive the rite.
2. **Substantial burden.** Prohibition of the sacrament makes the central rite impossible to practice.
3. **Compelling interest, least restrictive means.** Under *Gonzales v. O Centro Espírita Beneficente União do Vegetal* (2006), the government must show a compelling interest in applying the law to *this* claimant, not merely a general interest in drug control. The Court granted an exception for hoasca, and later exceptions followed the same reasoning.
4. **Inconsistent enforcement.** Seeds of morning glory and Hawaiian baby woodrose, which contain ergine, are sold openly as ornamental plants. A government that tolerates this cannot easily claim a compelling interest in prohibiting the same compound when it is used sacramentally.

The existence of this website does not make kykeon legal to use. A website cannot supply sincerity. The question to ask yourself is whether you subscribe to the ideas presented here with genuine conviction. Could you explain them to a judge without having the site in front of you? Also, reciting them is not the same as holding them. Courts look at conduct over time. A consistent practice supports your claim. Pure recreation as your only motive undermines it.
