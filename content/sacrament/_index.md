Our sacrament is a modern reconstruction of the kykeon, the drink of the Eleusinian Mysteries.[^reconstruction] Aspirants must [join the church](/membership/) before using the sacrament.

[^reconstruction]: Ancient writers name the ingredients (barley, water, pennyroyal mint) but not what made the drink powerful, so every account of the original rests on inference. We chose this reconstruction for its function, not its provenance. A rite open to a wide public needs a drug that welcomes newcomers, and this one does.

The sacrament is currently in beta test. Members' subjective experience reports of using the sacrament are on the [home page](/#testimonials).

## Orientation

Kykeon is similar in effect to MDMA, but the euphoria extends far beyond the MDMA ceiling.[^mdma-ceiling]

[^mdma-ceiling]: MDMA works mainly by forcing stored serotonin out of neurons. The supply is finite, so the euphoria saturates at a moderate dose. Higher doses add mostly stimulation (dopamine, norepinephrine, heat, jaw clenching) and side effects rather than more bliss. Meanwhile toxicity climbs, and the margin between a euphoric dose and a dangerous one is small (hyperthermia and serotonin syndrome can be fatal), so there is no safe way to push past the ceiling.

We advocate doing this yourself: source your own materials, learn the elementary chemistry, and take the sacrament without a sober sitter, so that no one holds that power over you. Without a sitter, you must keep watch over yourself.

The steps, in order: [screen for contraindications](#screen-for-contraindications), [gather materials](#materials), [extract the ergine](#extract-the-ergine), then [take it](#take-it). Optionally, explore the [pharmacokinetics tool](#pharmacokinetics) to see what to expect.

## Screen for Contraindications

**This protocol does not risk ergotism.** Ergine belongs to the ergot alkaloids. But ergotism (a potentially fatal condition) requires chronic, heavy exposure far beyond the mild dose here. Ergine is also a much weaker vasoconstrictor than the alkaloids behind classic ergotism. Still, vascular sensitivity varies, so screen yourself first.

Ergine's usual complaints are nausea, sedation, and dysphoria. It can also raise heart rate and blood pressure and mildly constrict blood vessels, which can show up as cold or tingling fingers and toes. Anecdotal reports suggest ergine's own effects are absent while aldehydes are converting it, so these risks apply mainly if the aldehydes run out and ergine reaches the brain unconverted.

Do not proceed if any of these apply:

- Cardiovascular disease, including coronary artery disease, prior heart attack, angina, or uncontrolled hypertension
- Peripheral vascular disease or Raynaud's phenomenon
- Pregnancy, or trying to conceive (ergot alkaloids can contract the uterus)
- Breastfeeding
- Liver impairment, since ergot alkaloids are metabolized there
- Taking strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (certain macrolide antibiotics, azole antifungals, HIV protease inhibitors), which can raise ergot levels dangerously
- Taking triptans or other vasoconstrictors
- A personal or family history of psychosis

Stahl himself is a heart attack survivor, yet he reports no vasoconstriction when he uses kykeon. [His ALDH enzyme seems to be unusual](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/posts/kykeon-cold-case/), perhaps from years on a ketogenic diet, and genetics are not ruled out (he has submitted a saliva sample for whole genome sequencing). His experience may not transfer to you. Until we know why, do not treat it as evidence that the protocol is safe for a damaged heart.

Ergot alkaloids aren't known to be bioaccumulative. The liver breaks them down (mostly via CYP3A4), and anecdotal reports suggest the body clears ergine within days. Humans have no known delayed illness from an exposure that felt tolerable at the time.

## Extract the Ergine

Even beginning chemists will recognize this as a straightforward LSA extraction.

1. Grind the seeds to a fine powder in the {{< tooltip label="grinder" >}}Stahl adds, "crush the seed fragments by hand with pestle using a twisting downwards motion<br/>for a minute until they look more like dust fragments, don't be afraid to then pound the seed dust<br/>with the pestle to get it crushed as finely as possible."{{< /tooltip >}}.
2. In a shot glass, combine the seed powder with 30mg tartaric acid and the ethanol. Stir for ten minutes.
3. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes (1 hour is better), to let the seed fragments settle out of the solution.
4. Pour only the clear liquid into the baking dish. Discard any cloudy liquid and spent seed matter. Let a dehydrator or hair dryer carry the alcohol away, keeping the temperature below 50°C (122°F) and flames and sparks away, because ethanol is flammable.
5. Once the alcohol has fully evaporated, scrape the residue from the dish into an oily blob with a spatula or razor blade.

The extract's weight includes inert oils, not just ergine. Divide the blob into two halves.

**Do not drink alcohol while aldehydes are in play.** Ethanol is metabolized into acetaldehyde, a carcinogen and the main cause of hangover symptoms. The ALDH blockers suppress the enzyme that clears it, so acetaldehyde would linger. Make sure the extraction ethanol has fully evaporated before you ingest the residue. Also avoid fried foods while the blocker is active, because heated frying oil produces reactive aldehydes such as 4-HNE and acrolein that would likewise linger.

## Take It

Kykeon can be co-administered with [tetrahydroharmine](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/docs/psychoactive/thh/).

1. Practice meditation to ensure you'll meet the kykeon in the best possible frame of mind. Kykeon isn't like MDMA—indifferent to whatever mood you present.[^mdma] If you only feel buzzing stimulation (without euphoria), consider whether you prepared yourself sufficiently.[^stimulation]
2. Stir half of the ergine extract (25 seeds' worth) into a slurry of the barley grass and a squeeze of lemon juice. Take it with your ALDH blocker all at once. Wash it down with seltzer water.[^seltzer] Starting with half is the safe way in: With 25 seeds' worth of ergine, the worst that can happen is an ordeal you will recover from in 3-4 hours.
3. Wait 90 minutes.
4. If things feel promising, take the other half (ergine only, since the barley grass and ALDH blocker from the first dose are still active).
5. Sip water throughout.
6. If things go wrong (sedation instead of euphoria), move as much as you can to resist sedation and encourage your kidneys to clear residual ergine.[^kidney-clearance] Avoid sodium while you recover, because salt makes the body retain water. A hot shower can help persuade the bladder to loosen its grip.

Expect nothing for the first half hour, then a gradual rise. The peak comes near the three hour mark, and the euphoria tapers over the next 3-5 hours. With a blocker, the comedown is mild.

## Pharmacokinetics

What follows is a guess built from journey reports,[^pk-credit] implemented as a simulator to make specific predictions. Picture the body as three compartments. The **pre-barrier pool** (stomach plus bloodstream) holds ergine and barley grass aldehydes until they reach the blood-brain barrier. The **blood-brain barrier** either leaves ergine untouched or converts it into kykeon, depending on whether aldehydes are present.[^not-liver] The **brain**, behind the barrier, is where the subjective effects happen.

[^not-liver]: Metabolic conversions like this usually happen in the liver, but Stahl's self-reports can't be reconciled with that account. For most people, ALDH clears the barley grass aldehydes, so the kykeon effect fades and the ergine effect reasserts itself. Stahl's ALDH seems to leave the aldehydes alone (perhaps a legacy of his ketogenic diet), so he feels kykeon throughout and never feels ergine. Yet ergine is certainly in his bloodstream, and ergine can ordinarily pass the blood-brain barrier. That he feels none of its effects points to ergine being converted into kykeon as it enters the brain. How the barrier does it is unknown. We suspect an enzyme.

[^pk-credit]: Deepest, most fervent thanks to Hamilton Morris—intrepid psychonaut who marched fearlessly into the ergine-aldehyde unknown so the rest of us wouldn't have to. May his blood-brain barrier be forever blessed. Read the saga in [The Kykeon Cold Case](https://brambles.joshuapritikin.com/posts/kykeon-cold-case/).

Ergine takes about 30 minutes to get from the stomach into the bloodstream. Blood circulates past the blood-brain barrier repeatedly. Barley grass aldehydes get from the stomach through the bloodstream to the barrier fast, in about 5 minutes. Psychoactive effects depend on travel across the blood-brain barrier. Each pass converts ergine into kykeon only if aldehydes are present at the barrier. Otherwise ergine passes through unchanged. Aldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH) attacks aldehydes, gradually depleting them. Kykeon has a short half-life in the brain, 10 to 15 minutes. Ergine that enters the brain unconverted lingers there and drags on the euphoria. A blocker keeps the aldehydes available, so every pass converts ergine and you feel none of it. Residual ergine is largely cleared by the kidneys,[^kidney-clearance] and that requires drinking water. The simulator is an empirical fit that simplifies this physiology (it treats the barrier as a single pass, for instance), and many of the underlying mechanisms remain a mystery.

## The Case for an RFRA Exception

*RFRA is American federal law. Many states have their own versions, each with its own wording and case law, and other countries regulate ergine under different statutes and recognize religious exemptions differently, if at all. This page is not legal advice.*

The Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA) bars the federal government from substantially burdening sincere religious exercise unless it shows a compelling interest and uses the least restrictive means of furthering that interest. Ergine, also called lysergic acid amide or LSA, is a Schedule III substance under the federal Controlled Substances Act. We contend that this regulation, as applied to the sincere religious use of ergine in our church, merits an exception under RFRA.

### Precedent

In *Gonzales v. O Centro Espírita Beneficente União do Vegetal* (2006), the Supreme Court unanimously upheld an injunction protecting the use of hoasca. It required the government to show a compelling interest in applying the law to *this* claimant, not merely a general interest in drug control, and the government could not, partly because it already tolerated sacramental peyote. Santo Daime won a comparable case. In *Church of the Holy Light of the Queen v. Mukasey* (2009), a federal court in Oregon held that the government had failed to justify prohibiting outright the church's importation of its sacrament for use at its ceremonies. The government limited its appeal to the injunction's breadth and did not challenge that conclusion, and the Ninth Circuit narrowed the injunction to match it. The Ninth Circuit later called the church the prevailing party overall.

Our church has a [plugin architecture](/modular/). The interface is firmness under provocation, and any tradition that supplies such a test can fill the slot. For now we rely on Santo Daime for the test, and members may belong to both churches. Delegating the test to a tradition that has already won under RFRA carries less legal risk than defending a test of our own. If the church grows, a version of our own may one day be worth building.

### The argument in brief

1. **Sincere religious exercise.** The church practices a progression of escalating challenge: contemplative skill, the kykeon welcome, and the [test of character](/manifesto/#an-uncheatable-test-of-character). Membership makes this concrete, because the sacrament opens only after you certify that you can [sit for fifteen minutes without effort](/membership/). A church that practices this package exercises religion, and the claim does not rest on kykeon alone. This page covers the kykeon step. The book behind our [manifesto](/manifesto/) makes the case for the whole progression.
2. **Substantial burden.** Our sacraments are the mechanism, not decoration. The practice rests on their psychological effects, so prohibiting them guts the practice. Religions heavy in content beliefs, such as cosmologies and salvation narratives, keep their creed, texts, and community when a ritual substance is lost. Ours requires only process beliefs, such as that attention can be directed and inner change is possible, and the sacraments are intrinsic to that process.
3. **Compelling interest, least restrictive means.** The government bears this burden. As *O Centro* requires, it must offer evidence about this claimant, not generalized findings about drug control, and show that no less restrictive alternative would serve its interest.
4. **Inconsistent enforcement.** Seeds of morning glory and Hawaiian baby woodrose, which contain ergine, are sold openly as ornamental plants, as are other plants that carry DMT or ergine. A government that tolerates this cannot easily claim a compelling interest in prohibiting the same compounds when they are used sacramentally. The government may reply that it simply declines to enforce against raw plants. But *O Centro* treated the peyote exemption as undercutting the government's uniformity argument, and an interest waived for some users is hard to call compelling for others.

The government may argue that the question must be answered substance by substance, or that steps in the progression are optional. But each step trains the practitioner for the next, and the progression exists for access, not compulsion. A step is optional for those who can proceed without it and necessary for those who cannot. Removing it without a substitute burdens exactly the people who most need it.

The existence of this website does not make kykeon legal to use. A website cannot supply sincerity. The question to ask yourself is whether you subscribe to the ideas presented here with genuine conviction. Could you explain them to a judge without having the site in front of you? Also, reciting them is not the same as holding them. Courts look at conduct over time. A consistent practice supports your claim. Pure recreation as your only motive undermines it.

[^stimulation]: Stimulation signals you set the correct conditions for kykeon. Otherwise you would feel ergine sedation. You're feeling the kykeon but not meeting it fully. If you'd like to be thorough about missing it, here is a tongue-in-cheek guide to getting it wrong. Set your alarm way too early, like 4am. Roll out of bed groggy and sleep deprived. Don't comb your hair. Proudly keep pristine bed-head. Take ergine before you've fully woken up. Definitely don't practice meditation. [^mdma]: MDMA is used to treat people struggling with complex PTSD because it is indifferent to their initial condition. Even a deeply distressed person is met with warmth and openness.

[^quercetin-dose]: One serving (1g, or ½ teaspoon) of quercetin was reported to not block ALDH sufficiently, and the aldehydes were exhausted after 5 hours. [^seltzer]: Seltzer helps clear the barley grass taste and aftertaste. This is more than a nicety: A lingering grassy taste is a distraction that could contribute to nausea.
