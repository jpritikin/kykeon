title: Who Minds the Temple?
---

Every proper mystery cult has a hierophant. Ours is **Matt Stahl**, whose work inspired this church. Sadly, he is an absentee leader. Like Persephone in the season of her descent, he is elsewhere, and the faithful wait.

In the meantime, **Joshua Pritikin** is the *acting steward* (*epitropos*, for the Greek-minded). A steward keeps the lamps lit and the doors unlocked. A steward is not a hierophant, and does not pretend to be one.

When the hierophant returns, the steward will gladly step aside. Until then, announcements will come from the steward. [Join the list](/join/) to receive them.
