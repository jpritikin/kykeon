Not everyone found Stahl's original recipe worked for them, and that is worth knowing. We want to hear what the community has learned: what you read, what you reflected on, what worked, and what didn't.

**Please do not explicitly admit to having tried anything.** Write about ideas, scholarship, hypotheticals, a friend of a friend, or the experiences of others. Do not describe your own acts of preparation or consumption, even under a pseudonym. Submissions are reviewed by the steward before anything is published, and nothing is published without your approval.
