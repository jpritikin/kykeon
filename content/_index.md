title: Second Church of the Kykeon
---

*Chaire*, traveler. The first church of the kykeon was the Eleusinian Mysteries, which ran for roughly two thousand years. We are the second.

We use the kykeon to celebrate the discovery of a way to measure Self-leadership, and so to select trustworthy leaders. [Read our manifesto](/manifesto/) or learn about [the sacrament](/sacrament/). If you feel moved to join, there is [one simple criterion](/membership/).
