*Chaire*, traveler. You have found the porch of a small and earnest church devoted to the Eleusinian tradition: the ancient mystery rites of Demeter and Persephone, and the barley-based drink, the *kykeon*, at their center.

We are young, we are few, and we are still deciding what we are. This site will grow as the community does.
