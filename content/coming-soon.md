The scaffolding is up around the temple. Here is what the builders have planned. Nothing below is open yet.

## Advocates

Membership stays free and rests on one criterion: you can [meditate effortlessly for 15 minutes](/membership/). Advocates are friends of the church who choose to support it with a small annual gift. It buys the extras below.

## The Map of Fellow Pilgrims

Advocates may choose to appear on a map, showing only a rough area. You can ask to meet another pilgrim in person, and contact details change hands only when both of you agree. The map is off until you turn it on.

## Festivals

One-day kykeon festivals in a city near you. The sacrament will be shared there, as it was at Eleusis. Tickets will be named and non-transferable, so no scalpers get between you and the barley.

## Sacrament

For those too lazy to prepare it themselves, we aspire to sell ready-to-use sacrament following the model developed by sister church, [The Sacred Synthesis](https://thesacredsynthesis.com/).
